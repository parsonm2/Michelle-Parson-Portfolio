A little context...

I'm a UX researcher and designer who believes that people are experts in their own lives. The people who use the products and services we design for should be at the center of the design process. The best products are designed with discernment for the interdependent nature of the product's existence within the ecological context of the user's life. As designers, we have an obligation to shape our product for the people using it. This begins with a genuine curiosity about people, their behaviors, goals, motivations, ideas, attitudes, and the friction they encounter and navigate in every day life.

My work sits at the intersection of qualitative research and systematic design. I'm drawn to complex problems that sit in between interdependent societal systems, and how to design these systems with humans who use and live within them in mind to better serve underrepresented communities and use design as a tool to enhance the human experience.

Previously a Coach and Group Fitness Class Leader, my experience personalizing experiences for people has equipped me to design with a human-centered approach. Currently open to new collaborations.

My Process

A constant feedback loop rooted in iteration and human-centered inquiry.

Step 01

Research

Understanding the problem and the people facing it, gathering relevant context and data.

Step 02

Problem Definition

After getting more context, going a step further to properly frame the problem.

Step 03

User Journey Mapping

Before design exploration, taking the time to map the user's journey and stories.

Step 04

Design Exploration

Exploring various design solutions and seeking feedback from relevant stakeholders.

Step 05

Usability Testing

Carrying out usability testing with users to locate pain points and use feedback for iteration.

Step 06

Hand-off & Monitioring

Handing off all design-related assets to developers and monitoring relevant performance metrics.
